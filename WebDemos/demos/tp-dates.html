Tripous Web Demos

Dates

This demo covers the Tripous date helper functions. They work with native JavaScript Date values and provide validation, date creation, date parts, date arithmetic, comparison, start/end of day helpers, and culture-aware text parsing.

The arithmetic and clear helpers modify the Date instance passed to them. Use tp.DateClone() first when the original date must be preserved. Date display formatting is covered in the focused Format demo.

Checks, creation and parts

Use these helpers to validate dates, clone dates, inspect day/month information, and check leap years.

Arithmetic and mutation

AddYears, AddMonths, AddDays, AddWeeks, AddHours, AddMinutes, and AddSeconds modify the passed date.

Comparison and day boundaries

Compare two dates, test a date against a range, and produce start/end of day values for filtering and date-range logic.

Parsing and normalization

TryNormalizeDateText, TryParseDateTime, and ParseDateText parse ISO-like dates and culture-pattern dates.
