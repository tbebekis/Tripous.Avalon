tp.Splitter

The splitter bar sits between two panels and resizes the first panel while the second panel fills the remaining space.

The tp.Splitter is just a splitter bar.

Place the splitter bar between two divs and let it auto-config.

You may define the Panel1MinSize constraint. And if UseBothPanels is set to false, (true is the default), then you may define the Panel1MaxSize constraint too.

If UseBothPanels is set to true, the default, then you may define the Panel2MinSize constraint too.

Panel 1

This is the resizable panel.

Drag the splitter bar to resize it.

Panel 2

This panel fills the remaining space.

The same splitter can switch between vertical and horizontal orientation.
