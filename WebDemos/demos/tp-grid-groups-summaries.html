tp.Grid - Groups and Summaries

This demo uses a small deterministic table so group rows and summary rows can be checked by eye.

The grid is grouped first by Department and then by Region. Summaries are applied to Salary, Bonus, and HeadCount.

Expected totals: Salary sum 5400, Bonus sum 580, HeadCount count 6. Department totals are: Sales salary 3000, Support salary 2400.
