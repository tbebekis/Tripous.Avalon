tp.Dragger

tp.Dragger adds mouse-based move and resize behavior to an existing element. It is a low-level helper used by desktop-like UI pieces such as windows, notification boxes, and drop-down surfaces.

The constructor receives a mode bit-field, the element to move or resize, and optionally a separate drag handle. In this demo the whole panel can be resized from its edges, but it can be moved only from its caption. The dragger raises DragStart, DragOver, and DragEnd as custom Tripous events.
